How it works

Simple, no‑hassle bin cleaning that fits around your rubbish collection day.

Leave your bins out

After your normal rubbish collection, just leave your bins at the kerb or in your usual spot.

Step 2

We arrive with our unit

Our mobile cleaning unit turns up and gets to work—no need for you to be home.

Step 3

We deep clean inside and out, kill germs and remove odours with eco‑friendly products.

Fresh bins, zero mess

Your bins are left clean, fresh and ready to use. We take the dirty water away with us.
